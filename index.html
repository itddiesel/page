ABOUT US

Who We Are

Injec Tech Diesel (ITD) is a trusted diesel fuel injection specialist based in Brits, North West, providing professional diagnostics, precision repairs and calibration services for both modern and conventional fuel injection systems.

We specialise in Common Rail systems, VE Rotary diesel pumps, diesel injectors and petrol injectors. Every component is carefully inspected, tested and calibrated using professional equipment to restore optimum performance, efficiency and reliability.
